FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do the Pop-Up Image & Video Flashcards work?
You choose how often flashcards appear. While your child watches their favorite videos or plays games on the device, a cheerful image or video flashcard pops up right on screen, speaks the word clearly out loud, and closes when tapped—turning screen time into learning time!
Can I create flashcards with our own family photos and videos?
Yes! Inside Custom Flashcards, you can pick any photo or short video from your phone (like Mama, Dada, Grandma, the family dog, or a favorite toy), type the word you want spoken, and include it in your child's pop-up flashcards.
What is the difference between Object Naming and Action Narration?
Object Naming lets you point the camera at any everyday object (like a toy car or apple) to hear its name spoken clearly. Action Narration describes what is happening in the scene (like "The bear is sitting"), helping children learn verbs and full phrases.
How does the 7-day free trial work?
Every new subscriber gets 7 days of full, unlimited access for free via Google Play. Choose $9.99/month or $79.99/year (save 33%), and cancel anytime in one tap inside your Google Play Subscriptions settings.
Is Kid Speech AI safe and private?
Yes. All custom family photos, videos, and Parent Word Logs are stored privately on your device. Camera frames used for live narration are processed ephemerally in real time and are never stored as personal profiles.
