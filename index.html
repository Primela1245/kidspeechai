How does Kid Speech AI help my toddler learn new words?
Kid Speech AI turns your everyday environment into an interactive talking picture book. When you point your camera at a real toy, snack, or household object, the app names it clearly using 27 warm, friendly voices—and lets you schedule pop-up flashcards right over your child's regular screen time.
What ages is Kid Speech AI designed for?
Kid Speech AI is built for parents and caregivers to explore alongside toddlers and young learners ages 1 to 5—whether they are practicing their very first words, building action verbs, or expanding into two- and three-word phrases.
