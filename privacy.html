Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Privacy-First Summary: Kid Speech AI: Play & Learn (published by SD Government Solutions LLC) is designed with family privacy first. We do not require user accounts, we do not sell or share personal data, we do not show third-party ads, and all custom flashcards and word logs stay locally on your device.

1. Who We Are

Kid Speech AI: Play & Learn (com.kidspeechai.actionnarrator) is an educational mobile application published by SD Government Solutions LLC. Contact: ceo@sdgovernmentsolutions.com or ai@kidspeechai.com.

2. No Account or Personal Profile Collection

You do not need to create an account or submit your name, email address, phone number, or child's identity to use Kid Speech AI. We do not collect or store personal user profiles on external servers.

3. Local On-Device Storage

  • Parent Word Log & Schedules: All vocabulary progress logs, voice preferences, and daily flashcard alarm schedules are stored locally on your Android device.
  • Custom Photo & Video Flashcards: Any photos or short videos you select using the Android system photo picker are stored strictly inside your device's private local storage and are never uploaded to public servers.

4. Ephemeral Real-Time Camera & Audio Processing

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used only while you actively point the camera during a naming or AR movement session. Camera frames are processed ephemerally in real time over encrypted HTTPS (TLS) to identify the object or action and speak its name, and are immediately discarded.
  • Microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE): Used only when enabled by the parent to detect vocalizations and play encouraging audio feedback. Audio buffers are processed ephemerally in memory and are never saved as permanent voice recordings or used for advertising.

5. Subscriptions & Billing

Subscriptions ($9.99/month or $79.99/year with a 7-day free trial) are processed directly by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your credit card or payment account numbers.

6. No Third-Party Advertising

Kid Speech AI contains zero third-party advertisements, does not use the Android Advertising ID, and never sells or shares data with data brokers or ad networks.

7. Deleting Local App Data

Because all custom flashcards and word logs are stored locally on your device, you can delete all data at any time by clearing app storage in your Android Settings or uninstalling the application.

8. Contact Us

Questions about privacy? Email us anytime at ceo@sdgovernmentsolutions.com.
