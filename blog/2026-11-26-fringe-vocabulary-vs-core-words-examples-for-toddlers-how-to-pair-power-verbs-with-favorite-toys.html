Fringe Vocabulary vs. Core Words Examples for Toddlers: How to Pair Power Verbs With Favorite Toys

Published 2026-11-26 · Kid Speech AI Early Learning Team · 5 Min Read

✨ Quick Parent Takeaway

In speech-language pathology, Core Words are the 25 to 50 high-frequency 'power words' (verbs, prepositions, and pronouns like 'More, Open, Go, Stop, Help, In, Out, My, Want, All done') that make up 80% of everything we say all day, while Fringe Vocabulary Examples are activity-specific nouns ('Dinosaur, Excavators, Blueberries, Puzzle, Bubble, Pajamas'). Toddlers cannot build 2-word sentences out of two fringe nouns ('Truck banana' makes no sense)—they need the '1 Core Word + 1 Fringe Word' formula ('Push truck!', 'Peel banana!', 'Open box!'), which you can model daily with Kid Speech AI: Play & Learn ($9.99/mo or $79.99/yr with a 7-day free trial on Google Play)!

Why Knowing 50 'Fringe Nouns' Doesn't Automatically Create 2-Word Sentences

Many parents tell us: "My 2-year-old can name 40 different dinosaurs, construction trucks, colors, and shapes, but won't ask for help or speak in two-word phrases!" That happens when a child's vocabulary is 95% Fringe Vocabulary (specific nouns) and only 5% Core Vocabulary (functional action and power words). As soon as you introduce 15 core verbs and relational words, all 40 of those fringe nouns suddenly snap together into hundreds of functional phrases.

5 Ways to Pair Core Words With Fringe Vocabulary Examples During Daily Play

  1. 1. Use One Core Verb Across 5 Different Fringe Toys ('Open + ___'): Take the core word "Open" and pair it with 5 fringe nouns in 10 minutes: "Open box!", "Open book!", "Open door!", "Open pouch!", "Open car!"
  2. 2. Model First-Person Toddler POV Action Videos ('Verb + Object'): Every clip in Kid Speech AI's First-Person POV Action Video Library models a core action verb paired with a fringe object: "Roll the ball!", "Stack the blocks!", "Drop the coin in!", "Peel the sticker!"
  3. 3. Scan Your Child's Favorite Fringe Toys With 2-Second AI Camera Naming, Then Add a Core Word: When Kid Speech AI's live camera names your child's toy ("Train!"), immediately model a core word before it: "Go train!", "Connect track!", "Stop train!"
  4. 4. Turn Snack Time Into a 'Core + Fringe' Menu: Instead of just naming foods ("Apple, milk, cracker"), pair every bite with a core word: "More apple!", "Pour milk!", "Eat cracker!", "All done spoon!"

Frequently Asked Questions for Parents

What are 10 examples of fringe vocabulary vs. core vocabulary?

10 Core Word examples: More, Open, Go, Stop, Help, In, Out, Up, Eat, All done. 10 Fringe Vocabulary examples: Teddy bear, Red ball, Wooden blocks, Sippy cup, Banana, Puppy, Train track, Sticker, Playdough, Drum.

Should I teach core words or fringe words first to a late talker?

Teach them together in pairs! A toddler is most motivated by their favorite fringe items (bubbles, snacks, trucks), and pairing each favorite item with an easy core word ('Pop bubble!', 'Open snack!', 'Go truck!') builds both vocabulary and 2-word phrases simultaneously.
