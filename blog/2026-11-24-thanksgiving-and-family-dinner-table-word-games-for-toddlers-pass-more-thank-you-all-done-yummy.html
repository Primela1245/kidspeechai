Thanksgiving & Family Dinner Table Word Games for Toddlers ("More, Pass, Yummy, Hi Grandma, All Done!")

Why Previewing Relatives' Faces in Custom Flashcards Stops Holiday Shyness

When a 2-year-old hasn't seen Grandma, Uncle Carlos, or Cousin Maya in two months, walking into a noisy living room feels like meeting strangers. But when your toddler has spent 3 minutes a day seeing Grandma's smiling photo pop up on your phone in Kid Speech AI's Custom Flashcards while hearing "Hi Grandma!", their brain recognizes Grandma immediately at the front door!

3 Low-Pressure Holiday & Family Dinner Table Word Routines

  1. 1. Build a 6-Card 'Holiday Family & Favorite Dishes' Deck 2 Days Before Dinner: Snap photos of Grandma, Grandpa, Auntie, Uncle, and cousins (or pull them from your camera roll) into Kid Speech AI's Custom Photo & Video Flashcards. Review them together for 2 minutes each morning!
  2. 2. Coach Relatives on the 'Two-Choice Plate Offer' (Instead of 'Say Please!'): Ask grandparents to offer two small portions at eye level instead of quizzing: "Do you want roll... or apple?" That simple shift turns shy silence into instant pointing and talking!
  3. 3. The 'Cup Clink Cheers!' & 'Pass the Basket' Game (Unlocks: Cheers, More, Here You Go, Yum!): Toddlers love clinking plastic water cups across the table while shouting "Cheers!" and helping hand a bread roll to Daddy ("Here you go!").

Add photos of Grandma, Grandpa, aunts, uncles, and cousins into Custom Family Flashcards before holiday gatherings!
