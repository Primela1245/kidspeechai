What to Expect at 17 Months: The 30-Day Countdown to the 18-Month Checkup

Seventeen months is a fascinating transition stage: your toddler walks, climbs, points to show you what they want, and understands 50 to 100+ spoken words around the house, even if their expressive word count is still between 5 and 15 words. Adding just 2 to 3 new functional request and action words per week between 17 and 18 months puts your child right into the thriving 18-month milestone zone.

Frequently Asked Questions for Parents

How many words should a 17-month-old say?

At 17 months, most speech therapists look for at least 8 to 15 consistent spoken words (including animal sounds, 'Uh-oh!', and partial words like 'Ba' for ball), with many 17-month-olds saying 20 to 40 words as they approach 18 months.

What if my 17-month-old only says 3 to 5 words?

If your 17-month-old has strong eye contact, points to show you things, and understands simple directions ('Bring me your shoes'), you can often double or triple their spoken word count over the next 4 weeks by shifting to 2-choice snack prompts and First-Person POV Action Videos.
