Why Late Talkers Can Still Succeed at Potty Training

You do not need full sentences to potty train! A child only needs one or two reliable, high-confidence signal words—like "Potty" or "Go"—plus familiarity with the sequence of objects in your bathroom. When children practice naming the potty chair, soap pump, faucet, and towel during calm, non-rushed moments, telling you they need the bathroom becomes second nature.

3 Bathroom Vocabulary Games That Make Potty Time Fun

  1. Make a 4-Card "Our Bathroom" Custom Deck: Use Custom Photo & Video Flashcards to photograph your child's actual step stool, potty seat, foaming soap, and favorite towel. Hearing those exact items named across 27 friendly voices removes bathroom anxiety.

  3. Sink & Bubbles Object Scan: After every trip, take 2 seconds with Live Camera Object Naming to scan the soap bottle, water, and towel while repeating "Pump soap! Wash hands! Dry hands!"
