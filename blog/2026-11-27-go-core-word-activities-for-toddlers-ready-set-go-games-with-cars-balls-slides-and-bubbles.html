Why 'GO!' Is the Ultimate Starter Core Word for Late Talkers

Many late talkers hesitate to repeat dry test questions like "What color is this?" because there is no physical payoff. When you hold a pullback car at the top of a ramp, chant "Ready... set..." and pause with a big grin, saying "GO!" launches the car across the room! Your toddler immediately experiences that their voice controls the action.

Frequently Asked Questions for Parents

What if my toddler can't pronounce the 'G' sound in 'Go' yet?

Many 15- to 24-month-olds pronounce 'Go' with a front tongue tap like 'Do!' or an open vowel 'Oh!'. That is a normal developmental sound substitution called fronting—celebrate 'Do!' or 'Oh!' and launch the toy immediately!

Why do speech therapists always pair 'Go' with 'Stop'?

Pairing two high-contrast opposite core words ('Go, go, go... STOP!') doubles your child's functional control over play and teaches impulse control alongside speech.
