Why Morning Words Stick All Day Long

By late afternoon, toddlers are tired and easily frustrated. In the morning, however, their attention span is fresh and every step of the routine—opening the blinds, picking a shirt, pouring milk—happens in the exact same order every day.

Introducing 1 new daily vocabulary word during the morning routine gives you and your child all day to spot and repeat that word together!

6 Easy Morning Routine Word Games

  1. The "Wake Up Sun!" Curtain Reveal: Before opening the blinds, say "One, two, three... Open!" and greet the window together: "Hi Sun! Good morning tree!"
