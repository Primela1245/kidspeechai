Frequently Asked Questions for Parents

Why shouldn't I tell my 2-year-old 'Say: I want more milk please' when they only say 'Milk'?

Jumping from 1 word ('Milk') to a 5-word sentence ('I want more milk please') overwhelms a toddler's motor planning. Always use the 'Plus-One Rule': when your toddler uses 1 word, model a 2-word phrase ('More milk!' or 'Drink milk!').

Does 'Bye-bye Dada' or 'All done' count as a two-word phrase?

Notice that 'Bye-bye' or 'All done' by itself is learned as one single unit, so once your child combines it with a person or object ('Bye-bye Dada!', 'All done milk!', 'More ball!'), THAT is a true 2-word combination!
