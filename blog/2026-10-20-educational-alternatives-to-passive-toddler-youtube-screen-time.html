Why Toddlers "Zone Out" During Regular Videos

Every parent needs 15 minutes to cook dinner, answer an email, or fold laundry. But traditional toddler videos move so rapidly—with constant background music and scene changes—that young learners often tune out individual words.

Instead of fighting battles over turning off your child's favorite shows, what if their screen time automatically paused for 5 seconds of focused word learning every few minutes?

How Pop-Up Image & Video Flashcards Work
