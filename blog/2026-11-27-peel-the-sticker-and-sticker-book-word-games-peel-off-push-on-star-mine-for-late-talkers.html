"Peel the Sticker!" Fine-Motor & Sticker Book Word Games for Toddlers ("Peel Off!", "Push On!", "Where?")

✨ Quick Parent Takeaway

A simple sheet of large puffy stickers is one of a speech-language pathologist's favorite secret weapons because peeling a sticker off the backing requires adult help at first ('Help!', 'Peel!', 'Off!') and sticking it onto paper or body parts teaches body vocabulary and prepositions ('Push on!', 'On nose!', 'On hand!', 'More star!'). Why Stickers Create 20+ Natural Requests in 10 Minutes

When you hand a toddler a flat sticker sheet, the edges are hard for little fingers to lift—creating an instant, frustration-free reason for them to look at you and request "Help!" or "Peel!" And because a single sheet holds 15 to 25 stickers, you get 20 back-to-back repetitions of the exact same functional phrase.

5 Sticker Book & Body-Part Speech Games for Toddlers (Ages 15 to 36 Months)

  1. 1. Watch the 'Peel the Sticker!' First-Person POV Action Video First: Play the 15-second eye-level Peel the Sticker POV Video in Kid Speech AI so your toddler sees little hands lifting a golden star sticker and pressing it onto a page while hearing "Peel the sticker!"
  2. 2. Lift Just the Corner ('Start the Sticker') & Model 'Peel Off!': Bend the backing paper so one corner of the sticker pops up, hold it near your face, and say: "Ready... Peel! ... Peel OFF!" as your toddler pinches and pulls it free.
  3. 3. Play the Silly Body-Part Sticker Game ('On Nose!', 'On Hand!', 'On Shoe!'): Stick a star on your own nose, point with wide eyes, and say "Look! Sticker on nose!" Then ask your toddler: "Where next? On hand... or on knee?"
  4. 4. Chant 'Push, Push, Push ON!' When Sticking It Down: As your toddler presses the sticker onto paper, tap it rhythmically with one finger while chanting "Push, push, ON!" (great bilabial P practice!).

Frequently Asked Questions for Parents

What kind of stickers work best for 15- to 24-month-old toddlers?

Reusable puffy foam stickers or large dot stickers work best for young toddlers because the raised edges are easy for chubby fingers to pinch and peel after you lift the corner.

Pro tip: How do I make flat stickers easier for my toddler to peel?

Peel away the entire background waste matrix sheet from around the stickers first! Without the surrounding paper border, every sticker edge lifts easily when your toddler says 'Peel!'.
