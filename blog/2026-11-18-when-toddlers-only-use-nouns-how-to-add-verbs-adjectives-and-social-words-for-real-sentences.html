When Your Toddler Only Says Nouns ("Truck, Apple, Dog!"): How to Add Verbs & Social Words for First Sentences

Published 2026-11-18 · Kid Speech AI Early Learning Team · 5 Min Read

✨ Quick Parent Takeaway

If your toddler can name 30 to 50 objects ('Truck, dog, apple, ball, blue, three') but still doesn't combine words into 2-word phrases, check if their vocabulary is 95% nouns! Because you cannot glue two nouns together into a sentence (nobody says 'Dog banana!'), toddlers need Action Verbs ('Go, Open, Eat, Push, Sleep'), Social Words ('More, Help, Mine'), and Locations ('Up, In, On'). Why 'What's That?' Quizzing Creates Noun-Only Vocabularies

Think about the question parents and grandparents ask toddlers most often: "What's this? What's that? Who's that?" Every single time we ask "What's that?", the answer is a noun ("Ball!" "Cow!" "Bus!"). Soon, your smart toddler thinks talking is just a game of slapping name-tags on objects! The moment you stop quizzing for nouns and start narrating what the object is doing, verbs and two-word phrases bloom.

3 Daily Shifts That Turn Noun-Labelers Into Phrase-Builders

  1. 1. Retire 'What's That?' and Use the 'Noun + Action' Echo: Whenever your toddler points and labels an object ("Airplane!" or "Dog!"), don't just say "Good job, that's a dog!" Immediately echo their noun and attach a verb: "Dog running!", "Dog eating!", or "Airplane flying up!"

Frequently Asked Questions for Parents

My toddler knows the entire alphabet and numbers 1–20, so why aren't they talking in phrases?

Letters, numbers, shapes, and colors are memorized 'list nouns/labels'—a child can't use the letter 'Q' or number '7' to ask for help or share an idea! Focus 100% of your playtime for the next month on functional action verbs ('Open, Help, Go, Stop, Eat, Push') and social words ('More, Mine, All done').

How many verbs does a toddler need before combining words?

Speech-language researchers find that once a toddler has around 10 to 15 everyday verbs and action/location words alongside 35+ nouns, two-word combinations start emerging rapidly.

Can we still use Live Camera Object Naming if my child already knows lots of nouns?

Yes! Every time Kid Speech AI names a household object during a 2-second scan ('Cup!', 'Chair!', 'Shoe!'), make it a game where you and your child immediately do an action with it ('Drink from cup!', 'Sit on chair!', 'Put shoe on!').
