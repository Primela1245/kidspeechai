Frequently Asked Questions for Parents

At what age should a baby or toddler know all 100 words on the First 100 Words List?

Most toddlers build their first 10 to 25 words between 12 and 18 months, hit 50+ words between 18 and 21 months, and reach 100 to 200+ words with regular 2-word phrases by 24 months (age 2).

Do unclear pronunciations like 'Nana' for banana or 'Ba' for ball count on the 100 First Words List?

Yes! At ages 1 and 2, word approximations count 100% as real words as long as your child uses that same sound pattern consistently for that specific object or action.
