The Real Reason Toddlers Melt Down When You Turn Off a Video

Have you ever noticed that your sweet, happy toddler turns into a puddle of tears the exact second you turn off a YouTube video or tablet show? Pediatric researchers call this the passive screen-time trance: fast-moving cartoons lock a toddler's visual attention while their body sits completely still.

When you abruptly turn off the screen, their nervous system experiences a sudden drop—leading straight to a meltdown.
