Why 27 Voices Help Toddlers Understand New Caregivers

At home, a toddler gets used to the exact pitch and cadence of Mom's or Dad's voice. When they walk into a daycare room and hear a new teacher say "Grab your cup" or "Time for nap", the unfamiliar voice can temporarily slow down comprehension. Practicing core routine words across 27 natural male and female voices in Kid Speech AI trains your child's ear to recognize words no matter who speaks them.
