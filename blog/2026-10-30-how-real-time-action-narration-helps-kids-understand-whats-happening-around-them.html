Why Kids Need More Than Just Single Object Names

Learning the word "Cup" or "Dog" is a great first step, but the real world is always moving! Somebody is pouring water into the cup, the dog is wagging his tail, or a sibling is stacking blocks on the rug.

When a child hears ongoing narration of both the object and the action happening right in front of them, they don't just memorize isolated words—they understand what is going on around them at all times.
