What everyday words help a 3- to 5-year-old feel confident starting preschool or kindergarten?

Children thrive when they can name their everyday supplies (backpack, lunchbox, water bottle, jacket, crayons), classroom actions (sitting, washing hands, sharing, lining up), and simple self-advocacy phrases ("Help please", "My turn", "Bathroom").

How can we practice school words at home before the first day?
