Why 16 to 17 Months Is the Bridge from Babbling to Real Naming

Between 16 and 17 months, your toddler is walking, climbing, exploring every room, and understanding dozens of words you say throughout the day. While they may still rely on pointing or cute word approximations (like "ba" for ball or "nana" for banana), their brain is rapidly mapping sounds to real-world items.

At this exact window, the more times your toddler hears a single clear word paired with the real object they are holding, the faster that word moves into their spoken vocabulary.
