Why the First 20 Minutes Awake Are Pure Gold for Toddler Speech

Toddlers learn language fastest through predictable scripts. When you say the exact same short, cheerful 1- to 2-word phrases at the crib rail, the light switch, the bedroom door, and the high chair every single morning, your child quickly learns to anticipate—and fill in—the final word of each step.

The 5-Step Crib-to-Breakfast Word Script (Ages 12 to 36 Months)

  1. 1. At the Crib Rail ('Good Morning!', 'Hi Mama!', 'Up, Up!'): Before lifting your toddler out, smile, hold your hands out for 3 seconds, and model: "Good morning! ... Want UP? ... Up, up, up!"
  2. 2. At the Light Switch & Curtains ('Light On!', 'Open!', 'Sun!'): Carry your toddler to the wall switch and window. Let their finger flip the switch while saying "Light ON!" and pull the curtains back saying "Open! ... Hi sun!"
  3. 3. During the Morning Diaper & Shirt Change ('Off!', 'Arms Up!', 'Peekaboo!'): Pause with their fresh shirt on top of their head for 2 seconds and ask "Where's [Name]? ... Peekaboo! ... Push arm in!"
  4. 4. Walking to the Kitchen ('Open Door!', 'Down, Down, Down!'): Pause at the closed bedroom door until your toddler taps it or says "Open!", and chant "Down, down, down!" on every stair step.
