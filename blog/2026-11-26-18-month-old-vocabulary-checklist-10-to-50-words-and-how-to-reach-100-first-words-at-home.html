18-Month Vocabulary Reality Check: 10 Words, 50 Words, or 100 Words?

Parents searching for an 18-month-old 100 words checklist often worry that their 18-month-old is behind if they don't have 100 words yet. Take a deep breath: 100 spoken words is the average milestone for a 24-month-old (2-year-old), not the minimum requirement at 18 months! However, using the First 100 Words Checklist starting at 18 months is the single smartest strategy you can use—because it gives you a complete menu of the 100 easiest, highest-frequency words to model over the next 6 months.

Frequently Asked Questions for Parents

Should an 18-month-old say 100 words?

No—100 words is the average vocabulary size at 24 months (age 2). At 18 months, 10 to 20 spoken words is the clinical baseline, and 20 to 50 words puts your toddler well on track for the 100-word explosion!

What if my 18-month-old understands 100 words but only says 5 words?

Strong receptive understanding (comprehension) of 100+ words at 18 months is a wonderful foundation! Use First-Person Toddler POV Action Videos and the 3-second expectant pause during snack and toy requests to turn their passive vocabulary into spoken words.
