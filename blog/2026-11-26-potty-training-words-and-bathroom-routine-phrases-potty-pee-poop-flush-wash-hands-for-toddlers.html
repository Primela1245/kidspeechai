Why Sequence Scripts Make Potty Training Easier for Late Talkers

Going to the bathroom follows the exact same 6-step physical chain every single time: walk to bathroom → pants down → sit on potty → wipe → flush → wash hands. When you attach one short, punchy 1- to 2-word phrase to each step of that chain, your toddler learns the words through muscle memory rather than pressure.

5 Playful Ways to Teach Potty Training Words Before & During Potty Training

  1. 1. Model the 1-Word Signal + Patting Gesture ('Potty!'): Every time you walk toward the bathroom, tap your hip or tummy and say "Potty! ... Time for potty!" so your toddler has both an easy gesture and a spoken word.
  2. 2. Let Teddy Bear Practice First ('Sit Bear!', 'Bye-bye Pee!', 'Flush!'): Toddlers love being the boss of their stuffed animals. Sit a teddy bear on the little potty, make a funny "Pssss!" sound effect, and let your toddler say "Sit!" and "Bye-bye!"
  3. 3. Turn the Handle & Sink Into Rewarding Action Verbs ('Push!', 'Flush!', 'Wash Hands!'): Flushing the toilet and turning the faucet on are huge cause-and-effect rewards. Pause with your finger on the handle and say: "Ready, set... FLUSH!"
  4. 4. Build a 5-Photo Custom Bathroom Routine Deck in Kid Speech AI: Snap photos of your child's actual potty chair, toilet paper, flush handle, step stool, and soap pump in Custom Photo & Video Flashcards so they can tap and hear the sequence during calm playtime.
  5. 5. Pair Handwashing With First-Person POV Action Videos ('Wash!', 'Water On!', 'Dry!'): Model "Water on!", "Soap!", "Rub, rub, wash!", and "All dry!" at the sink every single visit.

Frequently Asked Questions for Parents

Can I potty train a toddler with a speech delay or late talker?

Yes! Many late talkers potty train right on time when parents pair a consistent physical signal (tapping the potty card or patting their tummy) with a simple 1-word target like 'Potty!' or 'Go!'.

What are the most important first words for potty training?

Start with just 5 high-impact words: 'Potty', 'Down' (for pants down), 'All done', 'Flush', and 'Wash'.
