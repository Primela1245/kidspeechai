How Two Words Become Three Words at 27–29 Months

Once a toddler is comfortable pairing two words together, adding a third word happens when they connect Who + Action + Object (like "Daddy kick ball") or Action + Size + Noun (like "Roll big ball").

Instead of quizzing your 27- to 29-month-old, the fastest way to spark 3-word phrases is to let them hear playful, real-time descriptions of what they are doing right now.

6 Playful 3-Word Sentence Games for 27- to 29-Month-Olds

  1. Turn Playtime Into Live Storytelling: Open Real-Time Action Narration inside Kid Speech AI and point the camera at your child’s toy car ramp or stuffed animal tea party so they hear clear mini-sentences in real time.
  2. Play the "In, On & Under" Hide-and-Seek Game: Hide a favorite toy bear and model location phrases: "Bear on chair!", "Bear under blanket!", "Bear in box!"
  3. Master 1 New Word Daily Across 3 Angles: Keep expanding your child’s noun bank with daily Pop-Up Image Flashcards that show front, side profile, and angled photos of real objects.
  4. Watch & Repeat with Pop-Up Video Flashcards: When a short action video pops up over screen time, expand the word together: "Dog jumping! ... Happy dog jumping!"
  5. Create a "What Is Family Doing?" Deck: Add short videos of family members eating, reading, or waving in Custom Photo & Video Flashcards.
  6. Follow 3-Word Movement Prompts in 3D AR: Use Interactive 3D AR Exercise and cheer together: "Clap your hands!", "Touch your toes!", "Jump up high!"

What speech skills develop between 27 and 29 months?

Between 27 and 29 months, toddlers begin linking 3 words together (such as "I want cookie", "Doggy is sleeping", or "Put shoe on"), using early pronouns like "I", "me", and "my", and asking simple "Where?" or "What’s that?" questions.
