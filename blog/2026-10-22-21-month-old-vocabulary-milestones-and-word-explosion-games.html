What Is the Toddler "Word Explosion" Around 21 Months?

Many parents notice that for months, their toddler learns just one or two new words a week—and then suddenly, right around 20 to 22 months, they start repeating new words every single day! Why does this happen?

Once a toddler collects a core base of roughly 30 to 50 everyday words, their brain realizes that every single object and action in the house has a name. At 21 months, giving your toddler interactive tools to discover names on demand pours fuel on that natural curiosity.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I help my 21-month-old move from 10 words to 50+ words?

Focus on naming objects your child interacts with every hour (snacks, shoes, bath toys, family faces) and use Kid Speech AI’s Real-Time Camera Object Naming and Pop-Up Flashcard Overlays to multiply daily word exposures.

Should a 21-month-old be learning action verbs too?

Yes! Learning action words like sitting, jumping, eating, and opening at 21 months is what enables toddlers to start putting two words together by age two.
