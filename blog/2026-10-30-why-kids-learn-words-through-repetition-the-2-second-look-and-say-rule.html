Why Repetition Is the Superpower of Childhood Language

Have you ever wondered why your child wants to read the exact same bedtime book 30 nights in a row, or asks you "What's that?" while looking at the family dog ten times in one afternoon? It isn't boredom—it's how a growing brain wires spoken words to real-world objects.

Every time a child stops moving, focuses on an object for 2 seconds, and hears its name repeated out loud, the connection between sight, sound, and meaning grows stronger.
