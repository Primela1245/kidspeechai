Why 13- and 14-Month-Olds Put Walking First (And How to Spark Words Next!)

Between 13 and 14 months, many toddlers channel all their energy into standing, cruising along furniture, and taking their first steps. It is completely normal for spoken words to grow gradually while gross motor skills take center stage!

The best way to encourage a newly mobile 13- or 14-month-old to talk is to join them on the move—naming the exact objects they carry around the living room.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many words do 13- and 14-month-olds usually say?

Most 13- to 14-month-olds use between 2 and 10 consistent words or sound approximations (like "ba" for ball, "uh-oh!", "mama", and "dada") alongside lots of pointing and waving.

Does learning to walk slow down talking at 13–14 months?

Toddlers often focus intensely on mastering walking around 12–14 months. Using active games like Kid Speech AI’s Camera Object Naming and 3D AR Exercise lets them keep moving while learning new words!
