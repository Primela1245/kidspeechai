What Changes at 25 and 26 Months?

Right after turning two, toddlers discover that words give them power to direct their day. Instead of just naming an object ("Cup!"), a 25- or 26-month-old wants to tell you what should happen next ("More juice!", "My shoe!", "Dada go!").

If your 25- or 26-month-old still relies mostly on single words, adding action verbs and multi-angle visual repetition is the easiest way to unlock two-word combinations at home.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many words do 25- and 26-month-olds typically say?

By 25 to 26 months (just past the second birthday), many toddlers use 50 to 150+ spoken words and begin combining two words together such as "More water", "Daddy shoe", or "Big truck".

How can I help my 25-month-old move from single words to two-word phrases?

Use the "Plus-One Rule": whenever your toddler says one word (like "Dog!"), repeat it back with one action or descriptive word added ("Dog running!" or "Big dog!"). Kid Speech AI’s Real-Time Action Narration and Pop-Up Video Flashcards model these natural combinations automatically.
