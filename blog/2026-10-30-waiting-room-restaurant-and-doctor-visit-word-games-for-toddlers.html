Turning "Waiting Time" Into Connection Time

Every parent knows the challenge of keeping a 1- or 2-year-old happy while waiting for food at a restaurant or sitting in a quiet waiting room. Because you are already sitting face-to-face with your child, these 10-minute windows are actually golden opportunities for quiet word play!

6 Calm Restaurant & Waiting Room Word Games

  1. Table-Top Treasure Scan with Camera AI: Place a spoon, napkin, cup, and crayon on the restaurant table. Let your toddler point to one and scan it with Real-Time Object Naming in Kid Speech AI to hear the word spoken softly!
  2. Turn On Every-5-Minute Pop-Up Overlays: If you hand your phone to your toddler at the table, activate a 5-minute interval in Flashcard Schedules so real-world 3-angle photos and action videos gently pop up over their video and close on their own after 6–8 seconds.
  3. Swipe Through Familiar Faces in Custom Cards: Toddlers love looking at family members and pets when they are in an unfamiliar waiting room. Open Custom Photo & Video Flashcards to name Grandma, Grandpa, and the family dog together.
  4. The "Under the Napkin" Peekaboo Game: Hide a spoon or toy car under a cloth napkin on the table and whisper: "Where’s the car? ... Open! ... Car!"
  5. Whisper Voice Imitation Game: Waiting rooms are the perfect place to practice whispering! Whisper "Hi Dada!" or "Big fish!"—toddlers find whispers irresistible to copy.
  6. Narrate Quiet Actions with Action Narration: Point the camera at your toddler coloring or drinking water using Real-Time Action Narration so they hear calm, descriptive sentences.
