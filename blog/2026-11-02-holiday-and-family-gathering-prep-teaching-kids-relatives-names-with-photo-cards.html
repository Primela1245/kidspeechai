Why Previewing Faces & Names Transforms Family Visits

To a 2- or 4-year-old, six months is a huge portion of their life. When relatives arrive for Thanksgiving or holiday dinners expecting immediate hugs and conversation, kids often hide behind a parent's leg simply because the faces and names aren't fresh in their daily routine.

Just 3 minutes a day with real family photos and short waving videos bridges that gap completely.

3 Steps to Build Your Holiday Family Deck in 5 Minutes

  1. Upload 5 to 8 Favorite Relative Photos: Add clear, smiling photos of grandparents, aunts, uncles, cousins, and even Grandma's dog into Custom Family Photo Cards.
  2. Add Short 5-Second Waving Videos: Ask relatives to text a 5-second video waving and smiling, then add it to Custom Video Flashcards with a label like "Grandma waving" or "Uncle Sam smiling".
  3. Practice Greeting Phrases Across 27 Voices: Let your child hear "Hi Nana!", "Grandpa!", or "Cousin Leo!" repeated across 27 natural voices during breakfast or car rides so greeting them in person feels like meeting a celebrity they already know!
