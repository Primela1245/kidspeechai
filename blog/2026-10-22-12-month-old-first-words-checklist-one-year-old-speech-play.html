What Counts as a "First Word" at 12 Months?

When your baby turns one, you don't need to wait for textbook-perfect pronunciation! Early childhood language specialists count a sound as a real first word whenever your 12-month-old uses it consistently, intentionally, and on their own to mean the same thing.

That means if your 1-year-old says "ba!" every time they roll their ball, or "uh-oh!" whenever they drop a spoon from their highchair, they are officially talking!

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it normal for a 12-month-old to only say "Mama" and "Dada"?

Yes! At 12 months, many babies say just 1 to 3 words alongside pointing, waving, and expressive babbling. Consistent daily naming play helps expand those first words over the next few months.

Why does hearing 27 different friendly voices help a 1-year-old?

Hearing the same core word ("Ball", "Milk", "Dog") across 27 warm vocal tones in Kid Speech AI helps a 12-month-old recognize the word clearly across different speakers.
