Why Playdough and Crayons Spark Instant Two-Word Phrases

Unlike battery-operated toys that do all the work while a child watches passively, playdough and crayons do nothing until your child acts on them. That gives you dozens of natural moments to model action verbs ("Roll ball!", "Squish flat!", "Open red!", "Peel sticker!") and descriptive words ("Long snake", "Sticky hands", "Sharp scissors").

3 Table-Top Art & Playdough Word Games

Frequently Asked Questions for Parents

Why do speech educators love playdough so much for late talkers?

Playdough is sensory-rich, repeatable, and naturally requires help (opening tight lids) and high-energy verbs ("Smash!", "Roll!", "Cut!"), which are much easier for toddlers to imitate than polite quiz questions.
