Why Toddlers Use Word Shortcuts (And Why You Should Celebrate Them!)

When your toddler points at the family pet and shouts "Do!" for dog, or holds up their cup and says "Cu!", that counts as a real, intentional word! Early talkers naturally simplify words while learning to coordinate their lips, tongue, and breath.

The golden rule at home is: Never make your toddler repeat the word "the right way" before giving them what they asked for. Instead, smile, hand them the item, and gently emphasize the missing sound in your reply!
