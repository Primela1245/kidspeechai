5 Key Markers: Late Talker vs. Broader Speech-Language Delay

Speech-language pathologists look at five core foundation skills before a child says a single word: (1) Receptive Understanding (following simple directions like "Bring me your shoes"), (2) Gestures (pointing, waving, nodding, showing toys), (3) Joint Attention (looking between a toy and your eyes to share excitement), (4) Motor & Sound Imitation (clapping, copying silly sounds like "Uh-oh!"), and (5) Play Variety (pretending to feed a bear or pushing a toy car).
