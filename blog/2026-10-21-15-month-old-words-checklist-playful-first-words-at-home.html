What Vocabulary Looks Like at 15 Months

Fifteen months is one of the most exciting transitions in early childhood. Your toddler is newly mobile, curious about every cabinet and toy basket, and beginning to realize that specific sounds refer to specific people and objects.

At this stage, "words" include not only clear spoken nouns like "Ball" or "Mama," but also consistent word approximations (like "ba" for ball), animal sounds ("woof"), and joyful exclamations ("uh-oh!").

Frequently Asked Questions

Do word approximations like "ba" for ball count at 15 months?

Yes! When a 15-month-old consistently uses the same sound ("ba" for ball or "muh" for milk) to refer to a specific item, early learning specialists count that as a real functional word.
