Why the Hours Spent at Home Matter Most

Even when a child attends a 45-minute weekly session, the other 99% of their waking hours happen at home—at the breakfast table, in the backyard, and in the playroom. Children learn words through repetition: hearing the exact object or action they are looking at named clearly dozens of times each week.

4 Zero-Stress At-Home Repetition Routines (10 Minutes a Day)

  1. Morning 2-Second Object Walk: Walk around the kitchen or bedroom together. Whenever your child stops moving and focuses on a cup, shoe, or chair for 2 seconds, Kid Speech AI compares the camera frames and speaks the word out loud!

  3. Voice-Swap Repetition (27 Voices): Let your child hear the same word repeated across 27 natural English voices to keep repetition fun.
