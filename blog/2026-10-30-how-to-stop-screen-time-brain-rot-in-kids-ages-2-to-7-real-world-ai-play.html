Why Passive Cartoon Loops Cause "Brain Rot" in Ages 2 to 7

Parents everywhere have noticed the same pattern: a happy, energetic child sits down with a tablet showing fast-paced cartoon loops, and within 15 minutes their eyes glaze over. When it is time to turn the screen off, meltdowns follow—and worse, the child hasn't practiced a single real-world word.

Many kids ages 2 to 7 naturally love technology and gadgets. The problem isn't curiosity about technology—it is passive 2D screen consumption that disconnects children from the physical room around them.
