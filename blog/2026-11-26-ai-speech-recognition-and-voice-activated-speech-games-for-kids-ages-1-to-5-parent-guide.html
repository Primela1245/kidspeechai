Why Adult Voice Assistants Frustrate Toddlers—And How Child-Focused AI Speech Play Works

When a 2-year-old says "Buh!" for bubble or "Mo!" for more into a standard smart speaker, nothing happens—because adult automatic speech recognition (ASR) demands crisp adult consonants. For a late talker, that silence extinguishes their desire to vocalize. In early childhood language development, the magic breakthrough happens when a child discovers vocal cause and effect: "When I use my voice, the world responds immediately!"
