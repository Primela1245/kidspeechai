18-Month-Old Not Talking Yet? 7 Playful At-Home Word Games That Spark First Words

Published 2026-10-20 · Kid Speech AI Early Learning Team · 5 Min Read

✨ Quick Parent Takeaway

To help an 18-month-old start using more spoken words at home, focus on high-interest everyday objects, repeat single words clearly in warm vocal tones, and turn passive screen time into interactive naming moments. Using Kid Speech AI: Play & Learn on Google Play lets parents point their phone camera at real toys for instant object naming and schedule pop-up photo/video flashcards over their toddler’s favorite videos.

Why 18 Months Is the "Word Explosion" Launching Pad

Around 18 months of age, toddlers understand far more words than they can say out loud. They may point to the pantry when they want a snack, bring you their shoes when they want to go outside, or babble with expressive intonation—all while using only a handful of recognizable words.

The secret to helping an 18-month-old bridge the gap from pointing to speaking is high-interest repetition in real-world moments. When a word is tied to something your toddler loves right now—like their favorite red toy car, a banana on their highchair tray, or a photo of Grandma—their motivation to imitate the sound skyrockets.

7 Playful At-Home Word Games for 18-Month-Olds

  1. The "Camera Magic" Toy Hunt: Sit on the floor with three of your toddler's favorite toys. Open Kid Speech AI and use Real-Time Object Naming to point the camera at each toy. When your toddler hears the cheerful AI voice name their actual toy, repeat the word warmly ("Car!").
  2. Custom Family Face Flashcards: Toddlers light up when they see familiar faces. Use Custom Photo & Video Flashcards to add pictures and short clips of Mom, Dad, siblings, and pets so your 18-month-old hears their names throughout the day.
  3. The 5-Second Expectant Pause: Before pouring more milk or blowing another bubble, hold the item near your face, say the word clearly ("Bubbles!"), and wait 5 seconds with a big, encouraging smile.
  4. Turn Video Time into Pop-Up Word Practice: Instead of letting tablet or phone time stay 100% passive, enable Pop-Up Image Flashcards and Pop-Up Video Flashcards so a friendly word card gently appears on schedule over their favorite show.
  5. Model Simple Action Sentences: When playing with a stuffed animal, use Real-Time Action Narration so your child hears simple action phrases like "The bear is sitting" or "Rolling the ball."
  6. Pair Movement with Sound: Launch Interactive 3D AR Exercise and copy the dancing, stretching 3D character together while saying "Jump!", "Stretch!", and "Go!"
  7. Rotate Friendly Voices: Hearing the same target word spoken across 27 friendly AI voices helps 18-month-olds recognize the word pattern no matter who says it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many words do 18-month-olds typically practice at home?

Every toddler develops at their own pace, with many 18-month-olds practicing anywhere from 10 to 50 everyday words—including animal sounds ("moo", "woof") and exclamations ("uh-oh!"). Daily play-based naming routines help build that list steadily.

Do animal sounds count as words for an 18-month-old?

Yes! When your 18-month-old consistently says "moo" when they see a cow flashcard or "vroom" when pushing a toy car, they are using a symbolic sound with meaning—a key building block of early vocabulary.
