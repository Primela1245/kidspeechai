Why Position Words Unlock Longer Toddler Phrases

Suppose your toddler already knows the word "Ball" and the word "Box". The moment they learn the word "In", they can combine two familiar nouns into a brand-new 3-word phrase: "Ball in box!"

Frequently Asked Questions

Which position words do toddlers learn first?

Toddlers usually learn "in", "on", "off", "up", and "down" first between 18 and 26 months, followed by "under", "out", and "next to" between 24 and 36 months.

Why are position words so helpful for building 3-word phrases?

Position words act like glue between two nouns! Once a toddler knows "in" and "on", they can immediately connect words they already know into 3-word phrases like "Ball in box" or "Bear on bed".
