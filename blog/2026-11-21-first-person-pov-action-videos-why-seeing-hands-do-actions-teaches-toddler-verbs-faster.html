Frequently Asked Questions for Parents

What are 'mirror neurons' in toddler speech development?

Mirror neurons are brain cells that activate both when a child performs an action with their hands (like clapping or pushing a car) AND when they watch someone else's hands perform that same action—creating a direct bridge between motor imitation and vocal imitation!

Why is 5 to 8 seconds the ideal length for an action video flashcard?

A short 5- to 8-second clip shows one single, crystal-clear action (start -> peak action + spoken word -> happy finish) without overwhelming your child or turning into passive binge-watching.
