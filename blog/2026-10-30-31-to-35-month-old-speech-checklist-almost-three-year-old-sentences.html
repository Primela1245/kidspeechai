What Happens to Speech Between 31 and 35 Months?

In the five months leading up to a child’s third birthday, vocabulary shifts from naming single items to telling mini-stories about what is happening. Almost-3-year-olds love describing actions, sizes, colors, and who owns what!

If your 31- to 35-month-old still speaks mostly in 1- or 2-word bursts, giving them rich visual-and-spoken action models helps them bridge the gap into 3- and 4-word phrases without pressure.
