Why You Can’t Teach Action Words with a Frozen Picture

Imagine showing a 20-month-old a still photograph of a rabbit in mid-air and saying, "Hopping!" To a toddler, that frozen picture just looks like a rabbit floating in space. Now imagine a crisp, 3-second real-motion video of a rabbit hopping across a clean white card while a warm voice says, "Rabbit!"

Early childhood language research consistently shows that young children map verbs and dynamic nouns much faster when they can see the real movement unfold in front of their eyes.
